About Lux Cor Games

Lux Cor Games is an American indie studio, and we're gamers first. We make the games we want to play: the ones we'd stay up too late for, tell our friends about and come back to. If it isn't fun for us, it doesn't ship.

We don't stick to one genre. So far that's meant a cyberpunk western, a back-room poker game, a quiet walk through empty places and a zombie-smashing chariot with a cross welded to the plow. Whatever we make next, it'll be made the same way: by people who love games, for people who love games.

Our games so far

  • Brimstone & Buckshot: Steel Chariot: zombie-smashing car combat. Coming soon.
  • Neon Noon: a cyberpunk High Noon visual novel. On Google Play.
  • No People, No Stories: an endless liminal ASMR walk through empty places. Free.
  • Neon Noon: Dead Man's Hand: the poker prequel to Neon Noon. On Google Play.
  • Vtube Live: a VTuber app for building your avatar and going live. In development.

And we're just getting started. More games are on the way, in genres we haven't touched yet.

How we work

Every game is our own: original stories and original worlds, built in-house. We'd rather ship a small game that's finished than a big one that isn't, and we keep working on our games after launch.

We make games for PC (Windows, Linux and SteamOS) and Android, and we test on the Steam Deck. Every game has a "report a problem" button, and every report gets read.
