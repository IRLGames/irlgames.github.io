Our AI policy

Fun comes first. Every tool we use, AI included, is there to make a better game. However a game is made, if it isn't fun, it doesn't ship.

For now, we're an AI games studio. Lux Cor Games is one person with a vision for the games, and AI tools help build them. All of our assets are made in-house, and almost all of them on our own computers: the art, the 3D models, the music and the sound are our own making. The few pieces that aren't, we'll name: some free public-domain (CC0) texture scans, a handful of free open-licence fonts, and our code, which is written with the help of a cloud AI assistant. As the studio starts making enough money to support more people, we'll bring on human help where it makes sense. Until then, we're primarily an AI games studio, and we'd rather tell you that plainly.

Questions about how we make our games: LuxCorGames@proton.me
