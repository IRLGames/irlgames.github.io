Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have a question about one of our games? We read everything.

Questions, help, game feedback and ideas: LuxCorGames@proton.me

Community

Talk to us and other players on our Discord server, or follow @LuxCorMedia on X for news. Trailers and videos are on our YouTube channel.

Reporting a bug from inside the game

Every Lux Cor Games game has a "report a problem" button. It sends us a short note and a screenshot of where you are, so we can see exactly what went wrong. See our Privacy Policy for what a report contains.

Deleting your data

Our games don't create accounts or collect anything automatically. If you sent a bug report and want it deleted, email LuxCorGames@proton.me and we'll remove it.

Refunds

Purchases and refunds are handled by the store you bought from (Google Play or Steam).
