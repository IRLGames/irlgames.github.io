Reporting a bug from inside the game

Every IRL Games Studio game has a "report a problem" button. It sends us a short note and a screenshot of where you are, so we can see exactly what went wrong. See our Privacy Policy for what a report contains.

Deleting your data

Our games don't create accounts or collect anything automatically. If you sent a bug report and want it deleted, email irlgamesnow@proton.me and we'll remove it.
