About this game

One night at the back table of the Xenon Lamp, where every hand is honest and every player is hiding something.

Play real Texas Hold'em, never rigged, while the story plays out one hand at a time. What you win loosens tongues; what you lose keeps them shut.

Three endings. Twelve decks to collect from Sable's drawer.

Features

  • Single-player story, 3 endings
  • Real Texas Hold'em, never rigged
  • Collectible card decks
  • Controller support and Steam Deck controls
  • Touch controls on Android
