Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

This policy covers all games by IRL Games Studio ("we"), including Neon Noon, Neon Noon: Dead Man's Hand and No People, No Stories, on every platform.

The short version: our games don't collect anything about you unless you choose to send us a bug report. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no accounts.

What stays on your device

Your saves, settings and progress are stored only on your device. We never see them.

Bug reports (only if you send one)

Each game has a "report a problem" button. Nothing is sent unless you write a report and press Send. A report can include:

  • the message you write, and your name or email only if you choose to add them;
  • a screenshot of the game screen at that moment;
  • the game's version number and the type of device or operating system;
  • where you were in the game, such as the chapter and the last few lines of story shown.

Reports are sent securely (HTTPS) to a private Google spreadsheet that only we can read. We use them only to find and fix problems. We don't sell or share them, and we delete them when they're no longer needed or when you ask us to.

Store platforms

Purchases are handled by the store you buy from, such as Google Play or Steam, under their own privacy policies. On Steam, achievements are recorded through Steam. We don't receive your payment details.

Children

Our games are made for adults and aren't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

To have a bug report you sent deleted, or to ask any question about privacy, email us at irlgamesnow@proton.me. We'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
