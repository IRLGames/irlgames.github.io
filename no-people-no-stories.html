About this game

No People, No Stories is a quiet, endless walk through the places in between: a sunlit poolroom, a parking garage in the rain, a platform where no train stops, a laundromat at 3 a.m., a hotel lobby two floors high, a night mall with its shutters down, an office where the lights hum and fail, a long black road in the fog, an Egyptian temple where light falls from the water far above.

Every place is built fresh when you walk in, and it never ends. There's nothing to win and no one to meet. Just the hum of the lights, the water, the rain, mixed the way you like it.

Eleven places, three times of day, and a sound mixer for each. One of them is Sundown, the district from Neon Noon, empty at 3 a.m., with the theme playing from a bar somewhere. Free, from Lux Cor Games.
